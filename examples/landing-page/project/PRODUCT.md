# Lumen Roasters

Small-batch coffee roastery in Da Lat, Vietnam, founded 2019 by two former farm agronomists.

- Sells single-origin Vietnamese arabica and fine robusta, roasted every Tuesday and Friday.
- Subscription: 250 g every two weeks, 210,000 VND, pause or cancel anytime.
- Works directly with 11 farms in Lam Dong; pays 30% above the commodity price.
- Cafe and roastery open to visitors 7:00–17:00, closed Mondays. 14 Hoang Hoa Tham, Da Lat.
- Current bestsellers: "Cau Dat Honey" (stone fruit, brown sugar), "Langbiang Natural" (jackfruit, cacao), "Robusta Fine" (dark chocolate, low acidity).
