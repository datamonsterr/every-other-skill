# Brief: corner-shop checkout app

A family runs a small neighborhood grocery store in Vietnam. The mother sets prices and buys stock; the children and a part-time helper run the till after school. Prices live in the mother's head and on a paper notebook. When she is out, the helpers phone her to ask prices, guess, or undercharge. At the end of the day nobody knows the exact takings or which items sold.

The family's eldest son is a developer and wants to build a phone app for them.

Known facts:
- About 400 products, roughly 60 sell daily.
- Most customers pay cash; some use bank QR transfers.
- Internet in the shop is unreliable.
- The mother is busy and has moderate smartphone skills.
